Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Developer: AbhishekTech · App ID com.abhishektech.routine

At a glance

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics.
  • We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Routine is a day planner made by AbhishekTech. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, Routine saves the following on your phone only:

  • Tasks, subtasks, reminders and your daily timeline
  • Habits and the days you ticked them
  • Notes and ideas you put in Dump
  • Water, steps and breaks you log
  • The profile you give during setup: first name, age and gender (all optional), and your app settings

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it.

Information we do not collect

Routine does not connect to any server run by us. It has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that collect personal data. We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Permissions

Notifications
Used to show task reminders that you turn on. They are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
Photos
Only when you choose a background photo for a share card. You pick it in the system photo picker. The photo is used on your phone to make the card and is not uploaded.

Sharing your day

When you tap Share on a day card, Routine creates an image and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app, such as Instagram or WhatsApp. That app's own privacy policy then applies to what you post.

Backups

Android may include app data in your device's own Google backup, depending on your Google account settings. That backup is managed by Google and your account. We cannot access it.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Start fresh to delete all tasks and logs. Uninstalling Routine, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the app saved.

Children

Routine is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
